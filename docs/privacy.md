# pikapik Privacy Policy

pikapik collects no data.

- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account.
- Your settings and Pet progress stay on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere.
- Keyboard, mouse and window events are handled in memory to run the feature you turned on. They are never stored or sent.
- If you turn on App Volume, the sound of an app whose volume you change passes through pikapik on your Mac while it plays. It is processed on the fly, never recorded or saved, and never sent anywhere. The microphone is never used.
- The only network use is the optional Speed Test, which contacts speed.cloudflare.com only when you press Check Speed, and Apple's StoreKit when you leave a tip.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dev-pikapik/pika-tools/issues
